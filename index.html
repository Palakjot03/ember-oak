FAQ

The questions we get asked most.

When will my order arrive?

We roast on Tuesdays and ship the same day. Most UK orders arrive Wednesday or Thursday.

Whole bean or ground?

Whole bean stays fresh longer, but we're happy to grind for your brew method. Just pick it at checkout.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes, pause, skip or cancel any time from your account. No fees.

Is your packaging recyclable?

Our bags are kerbside recyclable and our boxes are 100% recycled cardboard.
